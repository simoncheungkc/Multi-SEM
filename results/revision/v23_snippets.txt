[13]
Tobler's first law characterizes spatial dependence through continuous distance decay, whereby nearby observations tend to be more strongly related than distant ones (Tobler, 1970). Yet administrative, legal, and functional boundaries often intersect with this geographic gradient. A residential property in Greater Boston has contiguous geographic neighbours, but also belongs to a municipal jurisdiction that determines property tax assessment, zoning bylaws, and public school provision (Harrison and Rubinfeld, 1978; Gilley and Pace, 1996). Similarly, a publicly traded firm belongs to an industry group while also being linked to corporate peers through return co-movement. Standard spatial regressions traditionally condition on a single weight matrix  (Bivand et al., 2014; Bivand and Wong, 2018). A single spatial operator cannot simultaneously accommodate these distinct relational channels. Multi-SEM resolves this limitation by testing, screening, and combining candidate weight matrices within a unified model while enforcing a uniform sufficient condition for invertibility of the spatial filter.

[14]
Higher-order spatial autoregressive and spatial-error models have been studied since Anselin (1988) and Lacombe (2004). Empirical practice nevertheless remains dominated by the selection of a single weight matrix . Weight-matrix uncertainty has been treated by Bayesian model averaging (LeSage and Fischer, 2008; Piribauer and Crespo Cuaresma, 2016) and by penalized selection (Lam and Souza, 2020). Multi-SEM is an alternative pipeline: ex-ante structural pruning, design-conditional screening, -constrained Bayesian estimation, and precision-domain spatial interpolation. Section 2.1 places that pipeline in the higher-order literature.

[28]
Higher-order spatial autoregressive and spatial-error specifications have been part of the classical toolkit since Anselin (1988). Lacombe (2004) showed that conclusions about a substantive spatial effect can change when the specification moves beyond a single weight matrix, and Lee and Liu (2010) gave efficient GMM theory for high-order spatial models with autoregressive disturbances. Recent empirical practice nevertheless remains dominated by the selection of one matrix . Weight-matrix uncertainty has been addressed by Bayesian model averaging (LeSage and Fischer, 2008; Piribauer and Crespo Cuaresma, 2016) and by penalized selection (Lam and Souza, 2020). Multi-SEM does not claim a new mathematical model class. It provides one reproducible pipeline that combines ex-ante structural pruning, design-conditional screening, -constrained Bayesian estimation, and precision-domain spatial interpolation.

====

[29]
Bayesian model averaging across weight matrices (LeSage and Fischer, 2008; Piribauer and Crespo Cuaresma, 2016) treats competing matrices under a model-selection assumption in which one network is the true generator. Multi-SEM instead models coexisting, overlapping dependence channels together, through one additive disturbance.
